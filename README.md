# Climbing higher: your academic career path

Interactive slide deck for the Work Readiness Workshop, St Joseph's Vocational Training Institute (November 2026).

## How to open
Unzip, then double-click `index.html`. It runs in any modern browser with no internet connection and no installation.

## Presenting
- Next: Right arrow, Space or Page Down. Previous: Left arrow or Page Up. F: full screen. T: next trade.
- Change the trade at any time: the trade buttons at the top right of trade-specific slides, or the Trade menu in the bottom bar. Every trade-specific slide updates at once, and the choice is remembered. Eight trades are covered: automotive, building, carpentry and joinery, electrical installation, plumbing, fitting and machining, hairdressing, and fashion and design.
- Four starting levels: Junior Certificate, National Certificate, Apprenticeship and Workshop Apprenticeship. Tap a route on slide 2, or choose one on the framework or route slides; the route, next-step card and take-home message follow it.
- The bar at the bottom hides in full screen and comes back when you move the mouse to the bottom edge.
- Page view (`index.html?view=page`) shows everything as one scrolling page, which is easier on a phone.
- PDF: press the PDF button and choose "Save as PDF" in the print dialog (one slide per page).

## Interactive slides
Trade picker, a clickable qualifications framework (tiers, rungs and bridges) with level descriptors and a self-check, a fee calculator for Uganda Martyrs University, lifelong-learning timeline, six ways to keep learning outside a classroom, a comparison of training programmes (EDIC bootcamp, National Apprenticeship Programme, SENDEA solar training, NWSC water skills facility, Makerere fundis workshop and more), permit guidance for electricians (ERA) and other artisans (TVET Council), route finder by trade, degree year-skip chart, three doors into a diploma, grade checker, earn-while-you-learn path, three-plan timeline, loan pictogram, savings planner, a real climb, year calendar, next-step card and a take-home message.
